# SEO audit 2026-10-10

## Summary
First audit (no previous report in `content/seo/audits/`). 384 of the 385 sitemap URLs return 200 with self-referencing canonicals; the exception was the sitemap's own `/blog/` entry (308 to `/blog`), now fixed. The main gaps were missing titles/descriptions on `/aboutus` and `/apply`, `/blog` copying `/community`'s metadata, a stale `robots.txt`, no JSON-LD on the home page, and thin blog-post frontmatter: 36 of 41 posts still have descriptions under 120 characters and most have fewer than 3 internal links. The sitemap went from 217 to 385 URLs (172 → 337 approved teacher profiles).
