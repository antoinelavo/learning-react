The site is in good shape: all 393 live URLs in both sitemaps return 200 with a title, description and self-referencing canonical, every JSON-LD block parses, and the community board (new since the last audit) passes its sitemap, 404 and anonymity checks. This run blocked the three private community pages in `robots.txt` (they had no `noindex`) and fixed 5 more blog posts' descriptions and internal links (31 posts still need it).
