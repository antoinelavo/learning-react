Lighthouse's LCP breakdown shows about 960 ms of element render delay on the hero `<img>`, with TTFB and load times small. FCP is 0.9 s and TBT 30 ms, so part of this may be lab variance. Top opportunities: unused CSS (est. 33 KiB, LCP −300 ms), unused JavaScript (est. 50 KiB, LCP −300 ms), render-blocking requests (est. 120 ms). Suggested fix: re-check next week. If it stays above 4 s, look at what delays the hero render (CSS or the client JS that hydrates `#heroSection`).
